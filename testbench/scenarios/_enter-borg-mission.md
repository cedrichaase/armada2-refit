# Fragment: from launch to the first Borg mission's briefing

Included by scenarios that need a map with a KNOWN faction. A direct map launch
(`Launch: -nointro a2_borg01`) is faster (~30 s against ~150 s), but it deals a random
faction's HUD each time. Federation, Cardassian and Romulan all came up in one run, and
their panels differ in shape, so comparisons across resolutions measured different
sprites. The campaign mission is always the Borg's.

1. Launch the game.
2. Wait for the main menu.
3. Click the single player emblem.
4. Wait for the single player screen.
5. Click the Borg campaign.
6. Wait for "Werewolf Pack".
7. Click the first mission.
8. Click the mission OK button.
9. Wait up to 300 seconds for the briefing.
10. Wait 3 seconds.
