# Fragment: from launch to the first Federation mission's briefing, over unexplored space

Included by scenarios that need the in-mission HUD with a KNOWN faction against a flat
background. A direct map launch deals a random faction (see `_enter-borg-mission.md`);
the campaign mission is always the Federation's. Its HUD is the plainest of the four —
flat frames with few details — so a stray line along a panel edge shows at once.

The briefing opens over the fleet, in front of a nebula. It is closed, the minimap is
clicked far from the fleet (all of it unexplored at the start, so the camera lands over
the flat grey of the fog), and the briefing is reopened with the checkmark in the
command bar. The minimap takes no clicks while the briefing is open.

1. Launch the game.
2. Wait for the main menu.
3. Click the single player emblem.
4. Wait for the single player screen.
5. Click the Federation campaign.
6. Wait for "Invasion".
7. Click the first mission.
8. Click the mission OK button.
9. Wait up to 300 seconds for the briefing.
10. Wait 3 seconds.
11. Click "OK".
12. Wait 2 seconds.
13. Click the minimap fog.
14. Wait 2 seconds.
15. Click the objectives button.
16. Wait up to 30 seconds for the briefing.
17. Wait 2 seconds.
